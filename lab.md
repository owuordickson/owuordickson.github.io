<section class="section-grid" aria-labelledby="lab-about"><div><p class="eyebrow">Our focus</p><h2 id="lab-about">Knowledge from complexity</h2></div><div class="prose"><p>Owuor Lab brings together the research directions of Dickson Odhiambo Owuor: data-centric knowledge discovery, interpretable representations, and learning methods for scientific applications.</p><p>The work connects algorithm development with questions from microscopy, bioinformatics, disease surveillance, and scientific knowledge representation. Open research software helps make these methods reusable.</p></div></section>

<section aria-labelledby="lab-themes"><div class="section-title"><div><p class="eyebrow">Research themes</p><h2 id="lab-themes">Where we explore</h2></div></div><div class="lab-themes">
<article><span class="card-number">01</span><div><h3>Gradual patterns & time series</h3><p>Mining relationships in changing data, with efficient algorithms, swarm intelligence, and metaheuristic optimization.</p></div></article>
<article><span class="card-number">02</span><div><h3>Graphs & microscopy</h3><p>Graph-theoretic representations and analysis of structural information in microscopy images.</p></div></article>
<article><span class="card-number">03</span><div><h3>Knowledge representation</h3><p>Ontologies and knowledge graphs that organize scientific information and connect concepts across text and data.</p></div></article>
<article><span class="card-number">04</span><div><h3>Disease surveillance</h3><p>Text mining, thematic features, and deep learning for event-based epidemiological surveillance.</p></div></article>
<article><span class="card-number">05</span><div><h3>Reinforcement learning & bioinformatics</h3><p>Learning and optimization methods for biological computation and resource allocation problems.</p></div></article>
</div></section>
