<section class="section-grid" aria-labelledby="about-heading">
  <div><p class="eyebrow">A little context</p><h2 id="about-heading">About my work</h2></div>
  <div class="prose">
    <p>My research centers on gradual pattern (GP) mining: discovering relationships such as <em>“the more one quantity increases, the more another decreases.”</em> I develop and optimize GP mining algorithms and extend these methods to time-series analysis and Symbolic Aggregate Approximation (SAX) models.</p>
    <p>Through international collaborations, my work also spans graph-based microscopy image analysis, ontology-driven knowledge representation, deep learning for disease surveillance, and reinforcement learning for bioinformatics.</p>
    <p>Alongside research, I teach undergraduate and graduate computer science, supervise research students, and coordinate the Vertically Integrated Project (VIP) program at Strathmore University. My CV records a visiting scholar and academic affiliate appointment with the University of Michigan’s Kotov Lab from 2024.</p>
  </div>
</section>
